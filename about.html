Welcome to WEBSHIA Pathshala, your trusted online learning companion for NCERT solutions, chapter summaries, and exam-ready study notes.

Our goal is to make learning simple, clear, and accessible for every student. Many students find textbook language difficult to understand, so we provide accurate question-answers and easy explanations written in simple English to help them prepare confidently for their school exams.

What We Offer

  • Chapter Summaries: Quick overviews of poems and prose chapters for fast revision.
  • Free Study Material: 100% free access to notes anytime, anywhere on mobile or desktop.
